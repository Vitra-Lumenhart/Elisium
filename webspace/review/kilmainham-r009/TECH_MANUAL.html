Kilmainham R009 — Primordial materials

Review branch derived from R008. Scene metres: 3.15 m structural bands, 6.30 m gallery pitch, 35 stair risers per flight, four travel nodes. The R008 registry remains immutable; its curved cell asset is no longer loaded or placed. Flat cell module instances are inset to the apse chord and adjusted to chord width; blank and crown geometry remains curved.

Material pipeline

kilmainham-materials.js maps original triangle atlas regions to masonry, oak, iron, boarding, flags or recess, then builds grouped geometry with metric UVs. Procedural pieces get projection in world coordinates during batching. Original KayKit geometry and custom GLBs are preserved in the R008 registry. The grade maps scanned luminance detail to specified RGB targets with bounded contrast and subdued original chroma. Textures initialize after renderer startup; failure leaves colored materials and reports counts.

Source registry

  • Door timber: dark_wooden_planks (Poly Haven, CC0)
  • Boarding: wooden_planks (Poly Haven, CC0)

  • Floor flags: pavement_01 (Poly Haven, CC0)
  • Optional linen swatch: rough_linen (Poly Haven, CC0)

Character source: KayKit Adventurers (CC0). Runtime palette remapping uses source atlas pixels, not new garment UVs. Optional source GLBs can be vendored to characters/; optional source JPGs to textures/. Local first, external fallback. The browser material workbench exports each graded map, including the optional linen sample.

Ironwork

Full flight side handrails, lower supported bars, overhead cage starts at riser 12. Single set of bridge cage halves at 1.05 m target hoop spacing, gallery barrel modules target 1.22 m longitudinal spacing. No claim of real-world safety compliance or surveyed historic dimensions.

Verification

Review source only. Browser rendering, external asset CORS, character atlas appearance and the apse chord joints need human verification. In particular this revision uses diffuse maps only; normal/roughness maps and bespoke garment textures are subsequent work.
