Material pipeline

kilmainham-materials.js maps original triangle atlas regions to masonry, oak, iron, boarding, flags or recess, then builds grouped geometry with metric UVs. Procedural pieces get projection in world coordinates during batching. Original KayKit geometry and custom GLBs are preserved in the R008 registry. The grade maps scanned luminance detail to specified RGB targets with bounded contrast and subdued original chroma. Textures initialize after renderer startup; failure leaves colored materials and reports counts.

R010 weathering and lighting

weathering.js injects a continuous spatial dirt field into standard materials using world positions, including instance transforms. Damp concentrates near the ground, streaks accumulate beneath both galleries, and wear at door thresholds repeats at each occupied tier. Iron has localized oxide; glazing receives variable tint and opacity. Material groups retain their photographed maps and add luminance-derived bump (not a calibrated height map). Roof and bridge boards have distinct tint multipliers. Character surfaces are weathered when optional models arrive. Lighting retains the rooflight and uses lower, cooler daylight with modest warm fill. The workbench exports the base maps only; live world-space grime is applied separately.
