## Canon, limitations and permission

- Original Lewis-inspired interpretations and dialogue, never invented quotations from C. S. Lewis.
- The relationship between Aslan and Tash is not asserted to be identity. Shift’s political coercion and Emeth’s sincerity stay distinct.
- Educational models explain methods of inquiry, not probabilities of doctrinal truth.
- Casual actions do not require answers, rankings or quizzes.
- Parent world observes deliberately emitted effects and retains its own celestial clock.
